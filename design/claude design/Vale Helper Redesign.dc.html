Vale Helper · UI redesign

Art first, thumb reach, one sheet for every card.

Phones are 393×851 dp and interactive: filter, jump by cost, tap a card, move it between zones, undo, tick the checklist. Zone state is shared, so a move made in one phone shows up in all of them.

Find a card · 2 taps
1. Tap the family in the bottom dock.
2. Tap the cost on the right-edge rail. The card's art is now on screen. Tap it to read the English effect.
Move a card · 1 tap
Press and drag any card into Table, Hand or Field. In the card sheet, the zone buttons toggle: tap the active zone again to untrack. No undo is needed.
Cleanup stands out
The checklist is the only light, parchment-colored block on a dark screen, with a progress count. Next shows how many steps are left. No warning dialog.
