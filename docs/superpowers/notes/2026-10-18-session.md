### Today, on `rail-highlight` (committed f3ae41f, not merged)
- **Rail vs highlight (option 2)**: the rail number follows what fills the screen while you
  scroll (only on real user scrolls); the card highlight is only what you picked on the
  rail and stays while you scroll, until another cost or family is picked.
- Family dock spaced evenly (`spaceEvenly`), centred group on wide screens.
- Settings split: **Appearance** (theme, animation speed) / **Card gallery** (cards per
  row, flowing grid).
- **Animation speed** setting: Instant / Fast (default) / Normal — covers the card sheet,
  grid reflow, rail jump, highlight, drag-up snap, tab switching.
- **Card sheet**:
  - own drag handle inside the card (dragging it works); no close button (tap outside or
    flick down closes);
  - **drag-up details**: module setting `sheet.details: {sections: [{label, path}]}` —
    text hidden below the zone buttons, revealed by dragging the card up (arrow-only hint
    under the buttons, no text, no ink box). Generic: rulings, story, flavour.
  - **flick left / right**: module setting `sheet.swipe: {left?: zoneId, right?: zoneId}`;
    the card follows the finger, tints in the zone's colour, and on release past the
    threshold goes to that zone and the sheet closes. No snackbar / Undo (by request).
- **Inline symbols**: `:name:` in any player-facing text shows that symbol, one line tall,
  centred, with the text's own spaces (matched to the fan wiki). Adjacent icons get a
  2 px gap. Used in effects, sections, details, secondary line, checklists, text blocks.
- **Vale 1.1.0 data**:
  - Hae-tae `interactions`: "Only the value changes. Any card that requires a
    :stone_purple: still needs an actual :stone_purple:." (owner's ruling).
  - Every "red / blue / purple stone(s)" → `:stone_*:`; counts 1–4 repeated like the
    printed cards (`Earn :stone_red::stone_red:`).
  - Every "point(s)" → `:points:` using the wiki's `Symb_points.png` (downloaded with the
    owner's OK, now `images/symbols/points.png`), numbers kept (`Earn 6 :points:`).
  - `swipe`: left = Table, right = Hand; drag-up section "Interactions".
- Docs updated for all of the above (MODULE_SPEC, AI_INSTRUCTIONS, schema) and the
  validator checks `details` and `swipe`.
